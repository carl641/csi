One source

Engineering, fabrication, finishing and installation sit under one roof, so the complicated ornamental portions of a project stay with one company from the first drawing to the punch list.

01

Engineering

CSI offers structural computations of a design project using COSMOS/M finite element analysis software. Monumental stairs, canopies and guard railings, to name a few, are analyzed for the material sizes and connection details they need to meet loading criteria and code requirements.

Preliminary design assistance can save the designer time and money, and our engineering department’s CAD and MicroStation systems are compatible with most designers’ systems.

  • Structural computations
  • COSMOS/M analysis
  • Connection details
  • Preliminary design
  • CAD & MicroStation

02

Fabrication

Rails, stairs, canopies and metalwork are built in our own shop in Decatur, Alabama — custom fabricated, formed and stretch-formed to the designed profiles, with laser cutting in house on a 10kW TruLaser 3040.

  • Carbon steel
  • Stainless steel
  • Bronze
  • Aluminum
  • Nickel silver
  • Laser cutting
See laser cutting

03

Finishing

Work is painted and finished in our own shop to the specification — with any type of finish required, from mirror-polished stainless steel to a natural patina.

  • No. 4, 6 & 8 stainless
  • Powder coat
  • AESS paint
  • Patina
  • Blackened steel
  • Mill finish

04

Installation

Our own crews install what we build. To keep a project on schedule we constantly interact with all concerned parties through verbal and written communication, CPM scheduling and site visits.

  • CSI crews
  • CPM scheduling
  • Site visits
  • Single-source responsibility
