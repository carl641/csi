About us

Since 1967, Construction Services, Inc. has delivered single-source engineering, fabrication, and installation for complex architectural metalwork.

Our History

Founded by Mike Scroggins and incorporated on February 3, 1967, Construction Services, Inc. (CSI) began as a manufacturer of miscellaneous metals before evolving into a specialized metal fabricator.

As a NOMMA member with a history of industry awards and a 100% contract fulfillment rate since inception, CSI delivers proven reliability to commercial partners.

Our products include

Rail Systems Ornamental and functional railing solutions including glass, stainless steel, cable, ornamental balustrades, and custom handrails. Stair Systems Integrated feature stairs engineered as unified structural assemblies, including straight-run, curved, spiral, elliptical, and center stringer designs. Architectural Metalwork Decorative and specialized building elements encompassing column covers, custom wall panels, custom light fixtures, torchères, gazebos, trellises, and sunshades. Canopies Walkway and entry canopies structurally engineered for environmental loads, fabricated to precise architectural intent, and installed by our own crews. Laser Cutting Perforated panels, screens and custom profiles, cut in our own shop on a TruLaser 3040 — from sheet, plate, perforated stock and tube.

Engineering capabilities

Certified Calculations

To ensure absolute safety and code compliance, we partner with a dedicated third-party engineering firm to handle all project calculations. Complex structures—such as monumental stairs, canopies, and railing systems—are independently analyzed and stamped to meet your specific project loading criteria and local building codes.

Preliminary Design Assistance

Providing preliminary design assistance allows us to save designers and architects substantial time and expense during the development process. Our engineering and design department utilizes advanced software platforms—including AutoCAD, MicroStation, SolidWorks, and TruTops Boost—to ensure complete compatibility with your team’s existing systems. Partnering with us early in the design phase ensures a seamless transition from concept to fabrication while preventing costly conflicts on the job site.
