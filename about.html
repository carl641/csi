What we do

We routinely handle projects large and small, from an individual gazebo to rail systems for an entire building complex.

01 Architectural metalwork Engineering and fabrication experience across a wide range of materials and finishes, from one source. 02 Stair systems Monumental stairs built as a single assembly, structure and rail detailed together. 03 Rail systems Rail systems for an entire building complex, or a single run of ornamental guard. 04 Canopies Entrance and walkway canopies fabricated to the architect’s intent and installed by our own crews. 05 Laser cutting In-house laser cutting for perforated panels, screens and custom profiles.

Single source responsibility

One source for the ornamental package

We also handle single projects that include a wide variety of different elements such as rails, monumental stairs, wall cladding, light fixtures, column covers and more.

Engineering, fabrication and installation sit together, so the rails, stairs, cladding and fixtures on a job are detailed by the people who will build them.

That single source responsibility is what keeps the complicated ornamental portions of a project from becoming somebody else’s problem halfway through the schedule.
