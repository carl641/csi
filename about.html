1967

History

Construction Services, Inc. was founded by Mike Scroggins and has been incorporated since February 3, 1967.

Once established as a manufacturer, CSI concentrated on miscellaneous metals, i.e. steel stairs and railings. Over the years, CSI has evolved into a specialized metals fabricator.

Our products include

01 Rail systems 02 Monumental and radiused/spiral stairs 03 Architectural metalwork A wide variety, including, but not limited to, wall panels, torcheres, gazebos, column covers and decorative art designs.

The materials used include

  • Carbon steel
  • Nickel silver
  • Aluminum
  • Brass
  • Bronze

With any type of finish required.

We also offer

  • Glass
  • Wood
  • Casting
  • etc.

CSI is a member of NOMMA, and has earned numerous industry awards.

We have fulfilled all contractual obligations since our inception.

Engineering capabilities

01

Structural computations

CSI offers structural computations of a design project by using COSMOS/M finite element analysis software. Monumental stairs, canopies and guard railings, to name a few, are analyzed for material sizes and connection details required to meet loading criteria and code requirements.

02

Preliminary design assistance

The preliminary design assistance can save the designer time and money in the development process. Our engineering department uses CAD and MicroStation systems that are compatible with most designers’ systems.
