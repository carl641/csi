What we make

Four product lines, each detailed, built and installed by the same team — in carbon steel, stainless steel, bronze, aluminum and more, with any finish.

Rail systems

Ornamental railing systems, functional as well as decorative — custom designs or stock component systems, from a single run of guard to a whole building complex.

  • Glass railings
  • Stainless steel railings
  • Cable railings
  • Ornamental balustrades
  • Handrails

Stair systems

Feature stairs built as a single assembly, with the structure, treads and railings detailed together, in carbon or stainless steel and any finish.

  • Straight-run
  • Curved
  • Spiral
  • Elliptical
  • Center stringer

Metalwork

The ornamental metal on a building, large or small, straight or curved — from a single column cover to every decorative element on the job.

  • Column covers
  • Wall panels
  • Light fixtures
  • Torchères
  • Gazebos
  • Trellises
  • Sunshades

Canopies

Entrance and walkway canopies engineered for their loads, fabricated to the architect’s intent and installed by our own crews.

  • Glass canopies
  • Aluminum canopies
  • Walkway canopies

From one source

Engineering, fabrication and installation sit together, so every product on a job is detailed by the people who will build it.

Engineering

Structural computations with COSMOS/M finite element analysis, and preliminary design assistance in CAD and MicroStation.

Fabrication

Built, laser cut and finished in our own shop in Decatur, Alabama — painted, powder coated, patina, blackened or polished.

Installation

Installed by CSI’s own crews, with one company answering for the scope from the first drawing to the punch list.
