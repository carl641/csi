Entrance & walkway canopies

An entrance canopy is the first part of a building people walk under — it has to shelter them, carry its load, and look right doing it.

CSI engineers, fabricates and installs entrance and walkway canopies to the architect’s intent, from radial glass canopies to long-span aluminum systems. Every canopy is analyzed with COSMOS/M finite element software for the material sizes and connection details it needs to meet loading criteria and code, and our own crews install it.

What we build

Materials Carbon steel · Stainless steel · Aluminum · Glass

Painted steel, powder coat or polished stainless steel.

Glass & steel canopies

Radial or straight glass canopies on steel or stainless framing, glazed with spider brackets.

Aluminum canopy systems

Long-span aluminum canopies at any elevation and pitch, powder coated to the architect’s color.

Engineered & installed

Calculations, connections and installation by our own crews, from first drawing to punch list.
