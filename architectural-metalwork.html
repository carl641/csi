What we do

The ornamental metal that gives a building its character — wall panels, column covers, grilles, fixtures and one-off decorative pieces.

CSI handles the architectural metal scope from preliminary design through installation, whether it’s a single gazebo or every ornamental element on a building. Our engineering department works in CAD and MicroStation alongside the designer, and the work is fabricated and finished in our own shop in Decatur, Alabama.

01

Cladding & wall panels

Wall panels, column covers, beam wraps and stainless steel cladding, custom fabricated to the layout of the building — including TECU copper panels left in a mill finish to patina naturally.

02

Grilles, screens & fixtures

Decorative grilles, screens, smoke baffles, torchères and light fixtures, formed, stretch-formed or laser cut in house to the designed profiles.

03

Monuments & decorative art

Gazebos, memorials, monuments and decorative art designs — from bronze grillage on a curtain wall to laser-cut, spiraled ribbon banners in a hospital lobby.
