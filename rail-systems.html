What we do

Ornamental railing systems can be functional as well as decorative.

We build to custom designs or from stock component systems to fulfill your design specifications — a single run of ornamental guard, or the railings for an entire building complex. Guard railings are analyzed for the material sizes and connection details they need to meet loading criteria and code, and the same team that details them fabricates and installs them.

What we build

01

Custom & stock systems

Custom railings built to the architect’s drawings, or stock component systems where the specification allows — guardrails and handrails for monumental stairs, balconies, arena aisles and exterior patios.

02

Glass railings

Glass systems where the glass is the structural component, or just an infill panel. With infill glass, a multitude of structural supports are available: posts, button connections, glass brackets and continuous top and bottom rods.

03

Metal & cable railings

Stainless steel cable railings, one- and two-line rails, and ornamental balustrades with decorative infill, from simple and clean to fully custom.

Materials

  • Carbon steel
  • Stainless steel
  • Bronze
  • Aluminum
  • Glass

With any finish — from No. 4 and No. 8 mirror stainless steel to powder coat, paint, patina and blackened steel.
