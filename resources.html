Before you call

Common questions

Do you work outside Alabama?

Yes. CSI has built projects from Texas to Washington, DC, and a monumental stair for the United States Embassy in Moscow.

Do you provide the engineering?

Yes. We run structural computations with COSMOS/M finite element analysis software. Stairs, canopies and guard railings are analyzed for the material sizes and connection details they need to meet loading criteria and code.

What do you need to price a job?

Send the drawings and we will price the ornamental scope. Suggested: “PDF drawings and the project manual are best; send them at least a week before bid day.”Placeholder

Do you install, or supply only?

Our own crews install what we build, so one company answers for the scope from drawing to punch list. Suggested: “We also supply fabricated work to other installers on request.”Placeholder

Custom design or stock components?

Either. We build custom railings to the architect’s drawings, or use stock component systems where the specification allows.

What are typical lead times?

Suggested: “Most rail and stair packages ship 6–10 weeks after approved shop drawings; we confirm a schedule with every quote.”Placeholder
